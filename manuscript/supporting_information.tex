\section{Dataset composition and MACE evaluation details}

The DFT label set contains 273 configurations: 194 ionic-relaxation frames and
79 fixed-geometry site or interpolation-path frames. The original
correlated split contains 211 training, 31 validation, and 31 test frames.
Because images from a shared interpolation path can appear in multiple
partitions, this split is used only to quantify interpolation within the sampled
configurational domain. The grouped split keeps each path intact and contains 263 training,
5 validation, and 5 test frames. Each grouped validation and test partition
contains one configuration from each structural family.

The first-stage grouped-$E_0$ checkpoint gives force RMSEs of 19.6, 10.9,
13.7, 42.9, and 9.2 meV \AA$^{-1}$ for the single grouped test configurations
from pristine, monovacancy, divacancy, Stone--Wales, and Si$_4$--graphene,
respectively. These one-frame family values are not population error estimates.
The three additional fine-tuning seeds give internal validation force metrics
of 96.4, 107.2, and 48.4 meV \AA$^{-1}$ in the archived MACE logs. These internal
metrics are not combined with the external frame-RMS values in
Table~\ref{tab:si_mace_errors}.

Foundation-assisted $E_0$ estimation was performed over all 263 grouped
training configurations. The archived fit has full elemental rank (3/3) and
gives baseline corrections of -3.271318, -1.246324, and -1.280346 eV for Li,
C, and Si, respectively. These quantities are model baseline corrections, not
isolated-atom energies.

\section{PBE-D3(BJ) adsorption across preselected sites}

Three placements per family were selected from the preceding PBE fixed-site
ranking without using MACE predictions and were recomputed with the common
PBE-D3(BJ), slab-dipole-corrected protocol. All 15 adsorbate calculations and
their substrate and isolated-Li references passed the final electronic
convergence and finite-force checks. The PBE rank order is retained in every
family. All 12 non-pristine placements are more favorable than all three
pristine placements; the separation between the least favorable
non-pristine row and the strongest pristine row is 0.555 eV.

\section{Family-balanced DFT force benchmark}

The post-training benchmark contains five deterministic Cartesian perturbations
from each of the five converged family geometries. Its 25 members were fixed
without MACE energies, forces, or committee disagreement. All 25 PBE single
points reached the final electronic-convergence criterion and yielded readable
forces. Table~\ref{tab:si_balanced_force} reports RMSE pooled over all Cartesian
force components in each family. Frame-level values, DFT forces, source-output
hashes, deterministic seeds, and perturbation amplitudes are included in the
version-pinned data archive.

\begin{table}[H]
  \centering
  \caption{Direct DFT force benchmark on five predefined perturbations per
  family. Force RMSE is in meV \AA$^{-1}$ and the reduction is relative to
  MACE-MPA-0.}
  \label{tab:si_balanced_force}
  \begin{tabular}{lrrr}
    \toprule
    Family & MACE-MPA-0 & Fine-tuned grouped-$E_0$ & Reduction (\%) \\
    \midrule
    Pristine graphene & 484.7 & 275.4 & 43.2 \\
    Monovacancy graphene & 449.7 & 256.3 & 43.0 \\
    Divacancy graphene & 381.3 & 246.3 & 35.4 \\
    Stone--Wales graphene & 496.3 & 297.5 & 40.1 \\
    Si$_4$--graphene motif & 427.5 & 258.5 & 39.5 \\
    \midrule
    All 25 configurations & 450.0 & 267.5 & 40.6 \\
    \bottomrule
  \end{tabular}
\end{table}

Across all configurations, the energy RMSE decreases from 26.6 to
4.6 meV atom$^{-1}$, the Li-force RMSE decreases from 992.6 to
65.1 meV \AA$^{-1}$, and the substrate-force RMSE decreases from 432.3 to
269.9 meV \AA$^{-1}$. The fine-tuned frame-level force RMSE is lower for 23 of
25 configurations. The improvement in all five families demonstrates that
fine-tuning benefits the predefined local perturbations beyond the
five-configuration correlation-controlled grouped split. The remaining family force errors of
246--297 meV \AA$^{-1}$ delimit that conclusion and do not establish global
configurational or dynamical reliability.

Pooling force components by perturbation amplitude gives fine-tuned RMSEs of
79.2, 190.1, and 373.6 meV \AA$^{-1}$ at coordinate standard deviations of
0.03, 0.06, and 0.10 \AA{}, respectively. The corresponding MACE-MPA-0 values
are 475.8, 435.6, and 450.8 meV \AA$^{-1}$. Thus, the relative reduction changes
from 83.4\% at 0.03 \AA{} to 17.1\% at 0.10 \AA{}, quantifying the progressive
loss of the local fine-tuning advantage with displacement.

\section{Finite-temperature trajectory context}

The finite-temperature trajectories were used to generate displaced
configurations for DFT force testing. The short and seed-sensitive traces are
not used to determine diffusion coefficients. Each replicated cell contains
four Li atoms. After the 10 ps equilibration period, LAMMPS
\texttt{compute msd} was reset for the Li group with \texttt{com yes}; the
reported in-plane quantity is the four-atom mean of the squared $x$ and $y$
displacements after removal of Li-group center-of-mass drift. It is therefore a
finite-window displacement diagnostic rather than a diffusion estimator.
