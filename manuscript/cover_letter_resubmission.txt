Re: Resubmission of COMMAT-D-26-03061

Dear Dr. Liu,

Please consider our substantially revised Research Article,
"Machine-learning interatomic potentials for battery materials:
Defect-dependent lithium adsorption and local transferability on graphene," for
publication in Computational Materials Science.

We thank you and the reviewers for identifying that the original submission did
not present its scientific contribution clearly enough. We have reorganized the
manuscript around a direct materials question: how graphene defects and a local
Si4 motif alter dilute Li adsorption, and how those chemically distinct
environments affect the transferability of a fine-tuned foundation MLIP.

The revised manuscript now leads with the first-principles findings. Consistent
PBE-D3(BJ) fixed-geometry calculations with a slab dipole correction give
adsorption energies from -2.967 to -0.875 eV per Li across three preselected
placements per family. All 12 non-pristine placements are more favorable than
all three pristine placements, with a separation of at least 0.555 eV, and the
preceding PBE site order is retained in every family. A separate, independently
defined benchmark of 25 DFT-labeled perturbations shows that corrected fine-tuning
reduces pooled force RMSE from 450.0 to 267.5 meV A^-1, with reductions of
35.4--43.2% in every family and lower frame-level error for 23 of 25
configurations. Direct DFT force tests on high-displacement snapshots then
reveal a local-environment-dependent MLIP response: corrected fine-tuning
reduces the Si4 snapshot force RMSE from 310 to 113 meV A^-1, while the
monovacancy error changes only from 1133 to 1097 meV A^-1. This contrast identifies the
reconstructed monovacancy environment as the priority for targeted
training-data expansion.

Detailed split analyses, calibration results, path scans, trajectory traces, and
snapshot tables now appear in the Supporting Information, whose contents are
summarized in a dedicated manuscript section. The revised paper does not claim migration barriers,
converged diffusivities, or practical battery performance. A separate response
file addresses every reviewer comment and identifies the corresponding revised
lines.

The version-pinned code-and-data archive is supplied as Supplementary Data for
peer review and publication. It includes the curated data, author-trained model
checkpoints, source code, licenses, and a file-level SHA256 manifest. Both
authors contributed equally to this work.

Thank you for considering the revised manuscript.
