Graphene defects and a local Si4 motif produce distinct dilute Li adsorption energies and distinct MLIP transferability. Target-domain fine-tuning lowers the DFT snapshot force RMSE for the Si4 environment from 310 to 114 meV A^-1, while monovacancy errors remain above 1.1 eV A^-1, identifying a chemically specific need for additional training data.
